Goal and usage limits

A goal needs completion criteria and at least one total limit: attempts, deadline, or tokens. A Once goal uses its continuation interval; other goals use their repeat cadence. Zero disables an individual usage limit. Token usage is checked after a run and can exceed the budget during that run; runtime and deadline caps stop active processes.
